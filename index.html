      Questions, answered plainly.

      Nothing here replaces your own judgement. It is a second pair of eyes.

      Is Nesty free?

      Yes. It is free to use, there is no sign up, and the code is open source under the MIT license.

      Does Nesty save my listing or messages?

      No. The rule checks run in your browser. If “Also ask Gemma 4” is ticked, the text goes to our small server, which passes it to Google’s Gemini API to run Gemma 4, then sends back the result. Our server does not store or log it. Untick the box and nothing leaves your browser.

      Can Nesty prove someone is a scammer?

      No, and it never says so. It shows signs often seen in rental scams and quotes the words behind each one. It can be wrong, so use it to decide what to ask next.

      What does the AI do, and what if it is switched off?

      In the full app, Gemma 4 reads for subtler signs such as pressure, an odd story, or template wording. The rule checks always run first and appear straight away. Gemma 4 then adds what it finds a few seconds later. If the AI is switched off or not available, you still get every rule check, and the page tells you so.

      Where does the rent comparison come from?

      From SpareRoom’s Rental Index for Q3 2026, covering about 20 UK student cities. Nesty flags rent below 60% of the city average.

      Does it work outside the UK?

      Not yet. The rent data and the advice are written for renting in the UK. More countries are a good first contribution.

      What should I do if I think I have been targeted?

      Stop paying, keep every message, and report it to Action Fraud and to the site where you found the listing. Tell your university accommodation team too.
